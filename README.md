# La Poste — Logistics & Accident Impact Analytics

![Python](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.x-blue) ![Excel](https://img.shields.io/badge/Excel-Analysis-217346) ![Geospatial](https://img.shields.io/badge/Geospatial-Analytics-green)

## Overview

A data analytics engagement for **La Poste**, France's national postal service and one of the country's largest logistics operators, conducted as part of the **Business Deep Dive** module at **Albert School × Mines Paris PSL**.

The objective was to analyze national road accident data to understand the human and economic cost of accidents involving logistics workers, identify geographic and demographic risk patterns, and build a KPI framework for operational disruption tracking.

---

## The Problem

La Poste operates a vast delivery network across France. Road accidents involving drivers represent both a human cost and an operational disruption — yet the economic impact of each accident was not being systematically quantified. The team needed a data-driven model to understand this cost and the risk factors behind it.

---

## Dataset & Scope

- **Source:** French national road accident data (2023), EU VSI/VSSI injury valuation standards
- **Focus:** Age and experience profiles of drivers involved in accidents, geographic accident distribution, economic cost modeling
- **Key finding:** Young drivers (20–30 years old) represent **34% of all accidents** but only **10% of the population**

---

## Methodology

1. **Demographic analysis** — Age distribution of accident-involved drivers
2. **Risk factor mapping** — Correlations between geography, driver profile, and accident frequency
3. **Economic cost modeling** — Built accident cost framework using EU VSI/VSSI life value standards, incorporating:
   - Property damage costs
   - Productivity loss (sick leave + reduced capacity on return)
   - Medical & administrative costs
4. **KPI dashboard design** — Disruption tracking framework for high-risk zones
5. **Geospatial analysis** — Regional accident distribution and operational impact mapping

---

## Key Findings

- Young drivers (20–30) are **3.4x over-represented** in accident data relative to their population share
- Primary risk factors: lack of experience, vehicle condition, reckless driving behavior, social pressure
- Modeled total accident cost using EU life-value standards — capturing direct and indirect economic impact
- Geographic clustering of high-risk accident zones identified for operational prioritization

---

## Outputs

- Quantified economic cost model per accident in France
- KPI framework for real-time disruption tracking across high-risk zones
- Geospatial risk map of accident concentration by region
- Final presentation delivered to La Poste stakeholders (`MIX_Group_7-FINALE-BDD-Laposte.pptx`)
